# Roasted Salmon Belly

*From 朴's blog, 2026-09-10 (`blog/2026/09/10/Roasted_Salmon_Belly.md`).
The timing is deliberately approximate — see the note below on why.*

## Ingredients

- Salmon belly slices, thawed, **skin on**
- Coconut oil, for the foil
- Ponzu sauce, a little, for coating
- To serve: white rice and a pickled plum

## Method

1. Line the baking pan with one sheet of foil, then add a second sheet
   that's been deliberately **wrinkled**, so the skin doesn't have a flat
   surface to stick to.
2. Coat the wrinkled foil with coconut oil to further prevent sticking.
3. Lay the salmon belly slices **skin-side down**.
4. Coat the belly with a bit of ponzu sauce — it dissolves and traps the
   aromatic chemicals that cause the fishy odor.
5. Roast at **425°F** — about **15 minutes for thick slices, or maybe 10
   minutes for thinner ones**.
6. Consider the **broil** setting for the last 2–3 minutes for browning.
   Watch that last minute carefully to avoid burning.

## On the timing

The "15(?)" and "maybe" are intentional, not sloppy. The cook time
depends on things that aren't fixed in advance:

- thickness of the slices
- fat percentage of the meat (belly varies a lot)
- starting temperature of the thawed slices

So read 425°F / 10–15 min as the ballpark and judge by the fish: it's
done when it's opaque and flakes easily, and the broil finish is what
gives the browned top.

## Food Safety

Treat as any cooked fish: refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and eat
within about 3–4 days.
