# Coconut Curry Rice

*From 朴, 2026-09-25. Ingredient ratios are 朴's. Method note from 朴:
with refrigerated rice, mix the coconut cream and curry paste into the
cold rice first, then heat the whole mixture in the microwave — stirring
once before and once after. No mid-way stir needed; temperature and
texture came out right.*

## Ingredients

- 250 g cooked white rice
- 30 g coconut cream
- 15 g Singaporean Chicken Curry Paste

Ratio to remember: **250 g rice : 30 g coconut cream : 15 g curry paste**
(2:1 coconut cream to paste).

## Method

**With refrigerated rice:**
1. Put the cold rice, coconut cream, and curry paste together in a
   microwave-safe bowl.
2. Stir so the paste is evenly distributed through the rice.
3. Microwave until steaming hot.
4. Stir again before serving.

**With freshly cooked hot rice:**
1. Stir the curry paste into the coconut cream until smooth.
2. Fold the mixture through the hot rice until evenly coated.
3. Serve immediately.

(Cold and hot versions taste much the same — 朴 has done both.)

## Notes & Variations

- **Other curry pastes as variations** — Indonesian (e.g. rendang or
  kari) and other regional pastes are candidates. They differ in heat,
  sweetness, and salt, so adjust paste quantity to taste.
- Salt/sugar balance will move with the paste brand — taste and correct.
- The cold-mix-then-microwave route lets the paste and coconut cream
  warm through with the rice, so there's no separate bloom step.

## Food Safety

Cooked rice can harbor *Bacillus cereus*; refrigerate leftovers within
2 hours (1 hour if hot) and eat within about 3–4 days. Reheat to 165°F
throughout — which the microwave step should do anyway.
