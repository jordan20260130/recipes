# Sesame Tuna Sashimi

*From 朴, 2026-09-25. Practiced method: two dipping dishes — one soy
sauce with wasabi mixed straight in, one sesame oil. Dip the fish in the
sesame oil first, then in the soy + wasabi. 朴 tried it and this order
won.*

## Ingredients

- Tuna, **sashimi-grade** (saku block), thawed — ~100–150 g per serving

**Dish 1 — sesame oil**
- Sesame oil, in a small dipping dish

**Dish 2 — soy + wasabi**
- Soy sauce, in a small dipping dish
- Wasabi, stirred into the soy sauce

## Method

1. Thaw the tuna in the fridge, pat it dry, and slice against the grain.
2. Set out **two small dipping dishes**: sesame oil in one, soy sauce
   with wasabi stirred into it in the other.
3. Dip each slice in the **sesame oil first**, then in the **soy +
   wasabi**. That order is the finding.

## Notes & Variations

- **On mixing wasabi into the soy sauce:** the usual convention is to
  keep them separate (wasabi straight on the fish). 朴 mixes them
  anyway, and it works for him. Noting it so nobody "corrects" the
  recipe later.
- **Spicy oil** — an option: alongside the sesame oil, or in place of it.
- **Seaweed** — 朴's other candidate: nori strips, or serve with wakame.
- The sesame oil is the change that made this worth writing down; keep
  the dish shallow and add drops as needed.

## Food Safety

Use sashimi-grade tuna and keep it cold until serving. Eat it the same
day it's thawed, and don't leave the fish or the dipping dishes out at
room temperature longer than about 2 hours (1 hour if the room is warm).
